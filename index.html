The Rundown
1
Come with a plan.
Connect with your partner(s) on Slack before the event. Align on an idea — or a shortlist — so you're ready to build when the day kicks off.
2
Hit the ground running.
The build window starts at 9:00. If you already got a head start with your partner before the event, keep going. If you're starting fresh that morning, that works too.
3
Build more than one thing if you want.
Finish early? Start something new. There's no cap on what you can build — the only rule is that you have something to show at 3:00.
4
Use whatever tools you want.
Design software, AI tools, code, no-code... whatever helps you build the best proof of concept. No restrictions.
5
Polish Optional
It doesn't need to be totally polished. A working proof of concept and a good story is all you need.
